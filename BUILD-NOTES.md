Build notes for the Weirdest Job Title entry.

The entry is a single program, main.ts, that writes one job title to
/output/entry.txt. The title is stored as a constant in the program, so the
output is deterministic: every run writes the same text.

The first draft was "Senior Vice President of Waiting for Microwaves". It is
a real-sounding corporate title with an absurd, passive duty, and it reads
instantly. On revision, the title was changed to "Chief Apology Officer for
Self-Checkout Machines". The new version is stronger for this judge: the job
is even easier to picture (a person whose whole role is apologising on behalf
of a machine), the twist lands on the first read, and the specific object,
the self-checkout machine, is something every reader has been annoyed by.
Both titles are well under the 60-character limit.

The README carries the three make.fun badge lines from the kit template,
each wrapped in a link to the bounty page. The LICENSE is MIT and carries
its SPDX identifier line.
